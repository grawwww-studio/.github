## Grawwww

Small engineering studio from Spain. We make AI pipelines faster and build agent systems that deliver tested work.

**Open source**

- [ComfyUI-Ref2VA-VSA](https://github.com/Kablex/ComfyUI-Ref2VA-VSA): a ComfyUI node that adds sparse attention to MiniMax H3 reference-to-video: with LightX2V's 4-step LoRA a 5 s clip goes from about 650 s to about 190 s, and our node takes it to about 72 s warm and from 21 GB to 13.5 GB of VRAM on one RTX 4090 (benchmark in the repo).
